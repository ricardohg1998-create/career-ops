# Fixture: Principal AI Automation Engineer

Example Systems is hiring a Principal AI Automation Engineer to build internal tools that improve support, operations, and product delivery.

## Responsibilities

- Design evaluation-driven AI workflows for document processing, routing, and human-in-the-loop review.
- Build production integrations across CRM, ticketing, knowledge base, and analytics systems.
- Partner with product, operations, and security teams to ship reliable automation with clear quality gates.
- Create dashboards and feedback loops that measure latency, cost, accuracy, and user adoption.

## Requirements

- Experience shipping applied AI, workflow automation, or data products in production.
- Strong JavaScript or Python skills and comfort with APIs, queues, databases, and observability.
- Ability to explain trade-offs to non-technical stakeholders and document operational risks.
- Preference for candidates with portfolio proof, implementation case studies, and pragmatic delivery habits.

## Logistics

- Remote-friendly role with overlap in European working hours.
- Senior IC scope with mentoring expectations.
- Compensation discussed during recruiter screen.
